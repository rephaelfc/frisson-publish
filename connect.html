Frisson Publish · internal tool — only accounts owned by Frisson Football are connected here.

Start the publishing tool on our computer first, then connect the account you want it to post to. You'll be sent to the platform to log in and approve the requested permissions, then back here.

Connect TikTok account

What the tool will do with TikTok

  • Show the connected account's name and avatar so we confirm it's the right account (user.info.basic).
  • Post our own approved videos with their captions, using a privacy level TikTok offers for the account (video.publish), or send them as drafts (video.upload).
  • Read our own videos' views, likes, comments and shares (video.list).
