Frisson Publish

The internal publishing tool of Frisson Football.

Frisson Football makes original short football data videos: goalscorer races, title races, transfer records, career maps and crest histories. Frisson Publish is the private tool we use to publish our own finished videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, and to read back how they performed.

It is not a public service and has no public sign-up. It only ever connects to channels and accounts owned and operated by Frisson Football (currently the Frisson Football channels; future sister channels we create are operated the same way).

How it uses YouTube

  • Uploads one approved video per day per channel to our own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API: videos.insert).
  • Removes our own test uploads when needed (videos.delete) and checks our own uploads (videos.list, channels.list).
  • Reads our own channel analytics once a week (YouTube Analytics API) to compare which formats perform best.
