Frisson Publish

The internal publishing tool of Frisson Football.

Frisson Football makes short football data videos: goalscorer races, title races, transfer records, career maps and crest histories. Frisson Publish is the private tool we use to post our own finished videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, and to read back how they performed.

It is not a public service. It only ever connects to accounts owned by Frisson Football.
