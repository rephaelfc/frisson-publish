Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Frisson Publish is the internal publishing tool of Frisson Football. It only connects to accounts owned and operated by Frisson Football. It has no public users and does not collect data about other people.

YouTube API Services

Frisson Publish uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API). By using Frisson Publish you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What we access, use and store

  • Accessed: the basic channel info of our own connected YouTube channel(s) (channel name and ID), the list of our own uploads, and our own channel analytics (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained).
  • Used for: confirming the correct channel is connected before uploading, uploading our own videos with their titles and descriptions, removing our own test uploads, and comparing which of our formats perform best.
  • Stored: an OAuth refresh token and our own channel's performance numbers, kept only on Frisson Football's own computer. We do not store any other YouTube data.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or disclose any data obtained through YouTube API Services to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising. No cookies or tracking are used on this site or in the tool, and no information is collected from any device other than our own.

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok

The same rules apply to our own Instagram, Facebook Page and TikTok accounts: we only post our own videos to our own accounts and read our own performance numbers, stored locally and never shared.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

Stored performance numbers are kept while the tool is in use and deleted within 30 days of a request or of revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions); revoking stops all use immediately and the stored token is deleted.
