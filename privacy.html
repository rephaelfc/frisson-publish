Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Frisson Publish only connects to accounts owned by Frisson Football. It does not collect data about other people.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile info of our own connected accounts (name, account id) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos and captions to those accounts.
  • Performance numbers for our own posts (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time).

How it is stored

Access tokens and performance numbers are stored only on Frisson Football's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and nothing is used for advertising.

Google user data

Frisson Publish's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Revoking access in the platform's settings stops all use immediately; stored numbers are deleted on request.
